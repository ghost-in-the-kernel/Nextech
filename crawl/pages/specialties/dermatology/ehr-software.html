Features & Benefits

Dermatology EHR

Nextech's dermatology EHRs are built for the full spectrum of derm — from medical visits and biopsies to cosmetic consults and laser treatments. Simplify charting, support MIPS compliance, and keep high-volume days running smoothly.

Schedule a demo
Schedule a demo
​

Mobile and tablet-friendly charting

Access patient charts, review notes, and document encounters from any device — desktop, tablet, or phone. Browser-based access means no app downloads and the same charting experience whether you're at your desk or between exam rooms.
​

Customizable templates

Build charting templates tailored to your workflow, whether it's a full-body skin check, a biopsy follow-up, or a cosmetic consult. Each provider can maintain their own template library to match how they practice.
​

Charting with smart stamping

Quickly mark conditions on anatomical diagrams using point-and-click smart stamps. Ideal for documenting lesion locations, tracking changes over time, and tallying affected areas for more accurate clinical records.
​

Built-in photo management

Capture, store, and compare clinical images directly within the patient chart to support documentation, treatment planning, and cosmetic consultations. Track lesion progression with side-by-side views, and eliminate the need for separate photo management tools.
​

MIPS & regulatory performance monitoring

Track quality measures like biopsy follow-up, melanoma reporting, and BCC/SCC pathology turnaround directly within your workflow, helping your practice maximize reimbursement and avoid penalties.
​

Integrated labs & e-prescribing

Order labs and submit prescriptions for controlled and non-controlled substances without leaving the chart. Built-in drug and allergy interaction checks support patient safety and reduce manual steps.
​

HIPAA-compliant dictation

Use voice-to-text dictation to speed up documentation while maintaining patient privacy. HIPAA-compliant talk-to-text tools let you narrate notes naturally and reduce end-of-day charting.
​

Integrated telemedicine

Conduct secure, HIPAA-compliant video visits directly from the EHR. The intuitive telemedicine interface makes it easy to evaluate patients and simultaneously document the encounter in a single workflow, in real time.
