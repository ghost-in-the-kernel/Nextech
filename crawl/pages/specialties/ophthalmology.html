FAQ

Nextech's ophthalmology EHR & practice management platform is a true SaaS/cloud-based solution so you can use any device from any location at any time. We provide all the updates, upgrades, and system maintenance for our customers ensuring an extremely low IT lift for your practice.

Nextech’s platform is designed for scalability and capable of meeting new needs as your practice expands. Our solutions can handle multi-location and multi-provider scheduling as you open new locations and/or take on additional clinicians. 

Nextech’s ophthalmology-specific EHR platform considers each patient’s complex case and comorbidities. This empowers clinicians to document the way they think and switch gears mid-exam, rather than using a siloed, tree-style system.

The result is a clearer picture of the patient’s health and a clinician who can spend more time on the patient and less on the paperwork.

Nextech's ophthalmology-specific EHR platform checks government quality measures in real time rather than scrubbing the record after the exam has been finished.

Accurate documentation in the medical record is essential not only for quality care, but is required for reimbursement. Nextech's ophthalmology-specific platform is designed to ensure clean usable data so you can avoid documentation issues that can lead to negative payer audits.

Yes! Nextech's ophthalmology-specific platform  supports a unique “shared care” system that enables other Nextech users to share data between practices. For non-Nextech practices, we support sending CCDA information.
