Nextech's internship program is less about fetching coffee and more about providing authentic work experience to the business leaders of tomorrow. So, we're proud to celebrate National Intern Day and our amazing group of interns this year.

National Intern Day was launched in 2017 and this year falls on July 27. The special holiday has served as an opportunity for thousands of employers to commemorate and thank their hardworking interns in various fields, including the realm of medical software. The day brings national attention to the inspiring investments companies make to internship programs and interns themselves.

Nextech understands the significance of investing in emerging talent, and our robust internship program is proof. Each of the past 10 years, Nextech has brought on a team of interns. Throughout the program, interns work with multiple department heads, perform meaningful tasks that are impactful to our business operations, and learn about the value our products provide our clients.

At Nextech, our internship program goes beyond providing valuable learning experiences; it delivers real results. At the end of our summer program, we consistently offer our interns full-time positions, and we take great pride that many Nextech interns continue to develop their careers here. This includes team members across the organization, including Logan Ohm, who started as a marketing intern last summer and now helps organize and manage national events as Associate Trade Show & Event Coordinator.

“During my internship, I received a lot of experience—and was given significant responsibilities—at large trade shows and events. This really made me feel that I was making a significant impact at the company,” said Ohm. “It also contributed to how at ease I was on my first day in my full-time position.”

We have been fortunate to welcome a cohort of talented interns this summer who have so far left a mark on our team and the products we create. Let’s get to know them!

Matthew Levin

Matthew works with the R&D Quality Assurance team, assisting in the development of test software in C# using Selenium. He is pursuing a Bachelor of Science in computer science at Loyola University New Orleans, but he previously earned an associate degree in technology development and management from St. Petersburg College through a college high school program.

“So far, interning for Nextech is the best working experience I have ever had. All my co-workers and bosses are super nice and helpful, the work is challenging and interesting, and I have had a great time writing test software for Nextech.”

Matthew hopes to continue his career in software and development.

Kennedy Murphy

Kennedy is working with the People and Culture team. Alongside the Talent Acquisition team, she screens resumes, sources candidates, and attends interview screenings. She is also working on various projects focused on career pathing and employee career development. She graduated with a bachelor’s degree in communications from Florida International University.

“I have enjoyed working for Nextech since the day I started. From the structure, organization, and remote environment to each member of my team, my experience thus far has been great! I have learned a lot about the healthcare industry and gotten more familiar with software systems.”

Kennedy hopes to become a more developed professional in the field of human resources and is learning as much as possible to enhance her career opportunities.

Lily Martin

Lily works with the Marketing team, specifically in the Demand Generation team. She helps create campaigns to increase brand awareness and create sales opportunities for targeted markets. She is pursuing a bachelor’s degree in public relations at the University of Florida.

“Everyone at Nextech has welcomed me with a smile and helping hand. The skills and knowledge I have learned about software are incredibly beneficial to my future career goals. Being a part of this marketing team has showed me just how powerful a good marketing strategy can be for a company.”

Lily hopes to continue her career in software, specifically in the sales or marketing departments.

Brandon Ruel

Brandon works with the Learning & Development team. Thus far, he has been aiding in project management of the launch of a new learning management system. He is pursuing a bachelor’s degree in integrated business at the University of Central Florida.

“I have loved working at Nextech! Working with the L&D team has taught me the importance of employee growth and empowering their capabilities to develop their unique skills and talents.”

Brandon is still exploring career options and hopes to get as much experience as possible to better learn what he enjoys and thrive in the business world.

Nextech offers an inclusive internship program where participants receive real-world experience and make genuine contributions to our business operations. We hire interns for multiple departments and host information sessions for the interns to learn about our broader organization and how departments work together within Nextech.

To learn more about our team and career opportunities, head over to our careers page»
