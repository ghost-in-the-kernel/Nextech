The connected growth playbook

How ophthalmology practices create a more connected path from inquiry to treatment

Every patient inquiry is an opportunity, but disconnected systems, inconsistent follow-up, and limited visibility can make it difficult to guide patients from interest to treatment.

This playbook explores how growth-focused ophthalmology practices are improving patient engagement, creating more connected patient journeys, and uncovering opportunities across LASIK, RLE, premium cataract, dry eye, glaucoma, and oculoplastics service lines.
