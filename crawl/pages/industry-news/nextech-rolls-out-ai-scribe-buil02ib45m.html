Nextech Rolls Out AI Scribe Built for Specialty Physician Practices

Fierce Healthcare published an article highlighting Nextech’s rollout of Cora Scribe, an AI-powered clinical assistant built for specialty physician practices. The coverage emphasizes Nextech’s focus on moving beyond generic AI tools by developing technology designed to align with the complexity of specialty workflows.

The article notes that Cora Scribe is embedded directly within the EHR, capturing patient encounters and helping generate structured documentation while reducing administrative burden. It also points to Nextech’s broader AI roadmap, including plans to expand capabilities into areas like orders, medications, and follow-up workflows. As Fierce Healthcare reports, the launch reflects Nextech’s commitment to building a healthcare-focused innovation engine that improves efficiency, supports providers at scale, and helps practices operate more effectively while staying focused on patient care.
