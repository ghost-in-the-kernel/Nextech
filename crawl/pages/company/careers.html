Medical

Nextech is excited to offer three benefit plans to fit your needs. All plans include open access to providers through the United Healthcare network, which means you do not need a referral to see a specialist and preventative care is covered at 100% with no deductible or copay required.

Dental

Vision
​

Vision

Coverage is provided through Guardian. In-network eye exams can be as low as $10. Participants are provided with an allowance towards eyewear and discounts towards LASIK and PRK.
Health Savings Account
​

Health Savings Account

Nextech offers a Health Saving Account (HSA) through Optum Bank. This plan allows you to use pre-tax funds on qualified medical, dental, and vision expenses. In order to participate, you must elect to opt-in to one of our High Deductible Plans. To help assist in your HSA growth, Nextech will make a contribution to your HSA account.
Flexible Spending Account
​

Flexible Spending Account

Nextech offers the opportunity to set aside pre-tax dollars in our Flexible Spending program, administered through Discovery. Employees have 3 options so they can choose one that best fits their needs.

  • Health Care FSA: This can be used to pay qualified medical, dental, and vision expenses
  • Limited Purpose FSA: If you decide to enroll in an HSA and are interested in enrolling in an FSA as well, this is the plan for you. This spending account can be used to pay for qualified dental and vision expenses
  • Dependent Care FSA: This account can be used to pay for expenses such as nursery school, day care, and summer camp for children up to age 13 and dependent adults

It’s a fun way to stay active while connecting with colleagues across the organization.

Important Notice Regarding Potential Employment Scams:

We have been made aware of certain fraudulent schemes that have been conducted by individuals or organizations claiming to represent the Nextech recruitment team. Employment scams are becoming more common, and job seekers are often a target. If you’re looking for a new job, keep an eye out for scams.

Important things to know about the Nextech Recruiting Process:

  • All available Nextech jobs are always posted on our career site via Lever.
  • The Nextech recruitment team will never ask for any sensitive personal information such as your social security number, date of birth, financial information, driver’s license number or passport information over the phone or over email during the interview process.
  • Nextech does not extend unsolicited employment offers and the Nextech team does not collect any financial commitment from candidates as a pre-employment requirement.
  • Official offers of employment are sent to a candidate’s email address as an offer letter. We do not extend offers via text or through Linkedin.
  • We schedule video interviews through Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or our partner Criteria. We do not require you to install any applications to your computer, and we do not use chat services for interviews.
  • Our recruiters only email using @nextech.com addresses or email@example.com.
  • The Federal Trade Commission offers information on job scams and how to protect yourself on their consumer website available here.
